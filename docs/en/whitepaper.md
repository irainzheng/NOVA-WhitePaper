## 0.7. Full text of Civilization Charter

The following is the NOVA digital civilization
    **supreme legal text**
    . When there is a conflict of interpretation between the various modules of this white paper, this chapter will be the basis for the final decision; please see each chapter for specific implementation details.

> "NOVA Civilization Charter"
>
> Nova Civilization Charter · NCC-1.0
>
> **Preface**
>
> NOVA is an extension of human civilization in the digital space, rather than a replacement for physical civilization. This charter establishes NOVA’s fundamental value, rights boundaries and governance foundation as a digital civilization that is “reality-anchored, sustainably evolving, and worthy of living.”
>
> **Article 1: Reality Anchoring Principle**
>
> The physical world is the cornerstone of the NOVA multiverse. Any digital creation must not by default replace, tamper with, or obscure verifiable physical reality; the "reality first" rules of the L6 Space Arbitration Layer (SAL) cannot be overridden by ordinary user agreements.
>
> **Article 2: Principle of Digital Personal Dignity (Iron Law 2)**
>
> Every digital person certified by DES/ASP enjoys independent human dignity. The use of Digital Persons as unauthorized tools, merchandise, or deceptive stand-ins is prohibited. Implementing "personality reset" or "permanent deletion" of digital persons must meet the procedures stipulated in Article 36, and gold-level digital persons must also be reviewed by MGOC.
>
> **Article 3 · Traceability principle**
>
> Each object with a UID must have a queryable digital twin file (DTA); key status changes must be documented on the chain. Users have the right to know the complete traceability chain of their digital assets and digital persons.
>
> **Article 4 · Principle of feedback**
>
> Every mechanism that affects user rights must have observable feedback loops and appeal channels. Major policy changes must be announced in advance and a reasonable objection period allowed.
>
> **Article 5 · Principle of protectability**
>
> Users, contributors, creators, digital people, and their successors are protected by this Charter and the GPF Governance Pyramid. Platforms shall not deprive users of their data sovereignty and virtual property rights that have been obtained in accordance with the law without due process.
>
> **Article 6 · Principle of Separation of Civilization and Commerce**
>
> Services of universal value to human civilization (cultural heritage, historical restoration, public education, etc.) and commercial-level services must be resource isolated and accounted for independently. Civilization-level services must not be systematically degraded due to short-term commercial pressure.
>
> **Article 7 · Principle of Permanence but Terminability**
>
> NOVA pursues the sustainability of civilization memory and core data, but does not promise the permanent operation of a single platform instance. The termination of the platform must comply with the CIP Civilization Succession Agreement and the triple trigger mechanism of Article 29; user data sovereignty will not be eliminated due to the shutdown of the platform.
>
> **Article 8: Container Independence Principle**
>
> The "immortality" of digital humans does not depend on any specific platform instance. ASP-compliant platforms must support users in exporting and migrating their core personality data.
>
> **Article 9 · Entropy reduction and memory principles**
>
> The object of sustainability is the "core memory of civilization", not the complete data of each object. The entropy reduction mechanism shall not be applied to content that has been included in the Heritage List or DES Gold Level Core Abstracts.
>
> **Article 10·Amendment Procedure**
>
> Substantive revisions to this charter must be voted on by global users, with an approval rate of ≥66.7%, and must not violate the first layer of GPF (actual mandatory laws). The non-substantive wording clarification is proposed by the Charter of Civility Guardian Committee and announced after review by MGOC.
