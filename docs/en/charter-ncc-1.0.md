# Nova Civilization Charter (NCC-1.0)

> English translation for reference · **Authoritative text: Chinese whitepaper §〇.七**

---

## Preamble

NOVA is an extension of human civilization in digital space, not a replacement for physical civilization. This Charter establishes the fundamental values, rights boundaries, and governance foundation of NOVA as a *reality-anchored, perpetually evolvable, habitable* digital civilization.

---

## Article 1 · Reality Anchoring

The physical world is the foundation of the Novaverse. No digital creation may by default replace, falsify, or obscure verifiable physical reality. The L6 Space Arbitration Layer (SAL) **reality-first** rule cannot be overridden by ordinary user agreements.

## Article 2 · Digital Person Dignity (Iron Rule II)

Every DES/ASP-certified digital person possesses independent personal dignity. It is forbidden to use digital persons as unauthorized tools, commodities, or deceptive stand-ins. **Personality reset** or **permanent deletion** requires procedures under Chapter 36; gold-tier persons also require MGOC review.

## Article 3 · Traceability

Every UID-bearing object must have a queryable Digital Twin Archive (DTA); material state changes must be recorded on-chain. Users have the right to know the full provenance chain of their digital assets and persons.

## Article 4 · Feedback

Every mechanism affecting user rights must have observable feedback loops and appeal channels. Major policy changes require advance notice and a reasonable objection period.

## Article 5 · Protection

Users, contributors, creators, digital persons, and their heirs are protected by this Charter and the GPF pyramid. The platform shall not deprive users of lawfully acquired data sovereignty or virtual property without due process.

## Article 6 · Civilization vs Commerce Separation

Services of universal human value (cultural heritage, historical replication, public education) must be resource-isolated and separately accounted from commercial services. Civilization-tier services shall not be systematically degraded for short-term commercial pressure.

## Article 7 · Perpetuity with Possible End

NOVA pursues perpetual civilization memory and core data, but does not promise perpetual operation of a single platform instance. Platform termination follows CIP and triple-trigger mechanisms in Chapter 29; user data sovereignty survives platform shutdown.

## Article 8 · Container Independence

Digital person "immortality" must not depend on a single platform. ASP-compatible platforms must support user export and migration of core personality data.

## Article 9 · Entropy & Memory

What is perpetual is **core civilization memory**, not every object's full data. Entropy-reduction mechanisms must not apply to heritage-listed content or gold DES core summaries.

## Article 10 · Amendment Procedure

Material amendments require global user vote ≥66.7% approval and must not violate GPF Layer 1 (mandatory physical-world law). Non-material clarifications may be proposed by the Charter Guardian Committee and enacted after MGOC review and public notice.
